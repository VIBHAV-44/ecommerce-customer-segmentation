# Interview Walkthrough
- **Problem:** Understand customer value, repeat purchasing and customer experience.
- **Data:** Synthetic relational e-commerce data, clearly labeled for portfolio practice.
- **Preparation:** Validated IDs, filtered to delivered orders for completed-sale KPIs, calculated line revenue.
- **Analysis:** SQL for category/month/customer metrics; Python/Pandas for RFM.
- **Visualization:** Power BI pages for sales, customer segments and customer experience.
- **Limitations:** Synthetic data is not evidence of real customer behavior; descriptive relationships are not causal.
